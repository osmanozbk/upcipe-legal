Subscriptions

Apple handles App Store billing. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Privacy requests

For questions about your data, or an access or deletion request, email the address above. Do not include account credentials; we may need to verify your request securely before acting on backend records.

You can review or withdraw AI-sharing permission in Upcipe’s Settings → Privacy. Withdrawing permission stops new AI requests; it does not recall data already sent or cancel a subscription.

This support page is not a privacy policy. The app’s data notice is available in Settings → Privacy.
