Privacy Policy

Updated October 13, 2026. This notice explains Upcipe’s data flows, retention and your choices.

Who operates Upcipe

Upcipe is operated by Osman Özbek, an individual publisher. For support or privacy requests, contact osmann.ozbekk@gmail.com.

Data on your device

Your profile, saved recipes, recipe history, shopping lists, nutrition diary, privacy choice and timer preferences are stored locally. Device backups may include these records according to your Apple settings. Your nutrition diary is not automatically uploaded to our backend. You can edit your profile in Settings and remove recipes or list items in their respective screens.

AI features and your permission

With your explicit permission, recipe generation, Uppie chat and shopping-list AI send your input and relevant context through our Microsoft Azure backend to OpenAI. This can include ingredients, recipe snapshots, questions and recent answers, shopping-list contents, household context, language, units and profile preferences. Optional allergies and intolerances can reveal health information; religious dietary restrictions can reveal sensitive preferences. Recipe icon matching sends recipe titles and the icon catalog, not your full profile. We use pseudonymous identifiers for service safety and operation management. Recipe research can use OpenAI web-search tools, so requests may involve search processing beyond the model itself.

Other service and purchase data

App integrity, installation and pseudonymous identity records protect the service. Subscription product, status, expiry and protected transaction identifiers verify purchases with Apple. Usage counters enforce quotas; request outcomes and performance metadata support reliability and abuse prevention. We do not use these data for advertising tracking. We do not maintain a permanent server-side recipe history or Uppie chat archive, but short-lived encrypted response and clarification data can be kept for retries.

Food search

Food search first uses available local data. Remote queries and language can be sent directly to Open Food Facts, or through our backend to USDA FoodData Central. Our service can also use Google Cloud Translation to translate food-search queries. These searches do not send your full nutrition diary or allergy profile. Search/translation results can be temporarily cached. Google Cloud Translation is an API service, not the consumer Google Translate product.

Retention and deletion

Encrypted response replay and clarification content have a 15-minute validity window. Expired content is cleared by periodic maintenance rather than instantly when that window ends. General operation tombstones have a 24-hour retention criterion, while related processing records may need multiple cleanup runs. Content-free service telemetry and Azure operational logs use a 30-day retention setting. Cleanup failures or processing safeguards can delay physical removal.

Icon results are kept in bounded memory caches (positive results up to 30 days, negative results up to 24 hours); raw recipe titles and principals are not retained in that cache. Food result caches use up to six hours for positive results and five minutes for negative results; query translations can remain in bounded memory caches up to seven days. Memory caches can expire, be evicted or be cleared on restart.

Identity, installation, credential, usage and purchase records are retained as needed to operate and secure the service, manage subscriptions and meet applicable legal obligations. These records do not all share the short content-validity window. We review verified deletion requests individually and explain necessary exceptions; there is no promise that every account record automatically expires after a fixed short period.

Our database has a seven-day backup/restore window. Deleted data can remain in older backups during that window; this is not an exact 168-hour physical deletion guarantee. We require applicable verified deletion/restriction decisions to be reapplied before restored data serves users. Support emails are targeted for manual deletion 90 days after a case closes, except where a justified legal or dispute-related need requires longer retention.

Provider processing and international transfers

Our OpenAI integrations disable stored responses, and optional organization data-sharing settings are disabled. This is not Zero Data Retention and does not erase data previously shared. OpenAI may retain content for abuse monitoring and other applicable provider processing, including temporary cache or tool state. Other providers also apply their own policies. Processing is not guaranteed to remain in your country or exclusively within the EU. Review the provider information below for their practices.

Your choices and requests

You can allow, decline, review or withdraw AI-sharing permission in Settings → Privacy. Without current permission, new AI requests are blocked. Withdrawal cannot recall data already sent, cancel an Apple subscription or erase device records. Notification permission is managed separately in iOS Settings.

You can email us to request access, correction, restriction or deletion of service data, or ask privacy questions. We may need proportionate verification before exporting or deleting records. Do not email bearer tokens, App Attest proofs, signed Apple transactions, payment credentials or sensitive profile details. An app-generated request reference, where available, is a receipt only: it does not itself delete data, cancel subscriptions or grant access to records. Email remains available if your device is lost or the app cannot create a reference.

We aim to respond within 30 days, or sooner where applicable law requires; extensions or necessary exceptions will be explained. Rights and deadlines depend on your location. Local data is managed on your device, and Apple subscriptions are managed through Apple. We do not recreate expired user content from backups solely to fulfill an access request.

Support and website hosting

If you contact us by email, your mail provider and Gmail process the message. Opening external policy or legal links follows the destination service’s privacy practices. Our static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, third-party fonts or contact forms. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when data flows or service practices change. The date above identifies the current notice. Material AI-sharing disclosure changes require a new in-app permission decision.
